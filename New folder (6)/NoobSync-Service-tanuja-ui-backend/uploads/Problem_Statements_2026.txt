Problem Statements for 
Hackathon HackNow - 2026

The ServiceNow components and design elements outlined in each problem statement are provided purely as a starting point and 
source of inspiration - not as a mandatory blueprint. They represent one possible approach to solving the problem using the 
ServiceNow platform.
You are completely free and actively encouraged to explore the full breadth of the ServiceNow platform and bring in any modules,
applications, technical components, integrations, or Now Platform capabilities that you believe best solve the problem. Whether that 
means leveraging Now Assist (GenAI), RPA (Robotic Process Automation), Virtual Agent, Predictive Intelligence, Process Automation 
Designer, Integration Hub, App Engine Studio, or any other ServiceNow capability not mentioned in the problem statement - go for it.
The best solutions will be those that demonstrate creativity, technical depth, real-world impact, and innovative use of the ServiceNow 
platform - not those that simply check off the listed elements. Think beyond the list. The elements are a suggestion; your imagination 
is the only real constraint.

Background:
Universities enroll thousands of international students whose visa status, study authorization, and work permissions must be carefully managed to 
maintain SEVIS compliance with the U.S. Department of Homeland Security. International student advisors manage complex cases involving I-20 
extensions, OPT/CPT work authorization requests, travel signatures, status violations, and reporting requirements. These are currently managed 
through email, PDF forms, and a combination of SEVIS access and spreadsheets. Students are often confused about requirements and
deadlines, and advisors manage large caseloads without structured workflow tools.
Objective:
Design a ServiceNow international student services management platform that provides students with a clear portal for all visa and authorization 
requests, automates document review and advisor workflows, manages regulatory reporting deadlines, and gives DSO (Designated School 
Official) teams visibility into compliance status across all active international students.
Elements to include in Design:
•Student Services Portal: Guided request forms for I-20 extensions, OPT/CPT, travel signatures, and status inquiries
•Advisor Case Management: Case workspace with student immigration history, document storage, and action tracking
•Regulatory Deadline Management: Automated tracking of OPT application windows, travel signature expiry, and SEVIS reporting dates
•Document Review Workflows: Structured intake, review, and issuance workflows for immigration documents
•Notifications: Deadline reminders for students and advisors, request status updates, and required action alerts
•Knowledge Base: Comprehensive guides on visa types, work authorization, travel requirements, and enrollment maintenance
•Compliance Dashboard: Active international student status overview, upcoming deadlines, pending requests, and advisor workload
•ServiceNow AI Agents

Background:
Local government offices receive thousands of citizen requests annually for building permits, zoning inquiries, noise complaints, pothole reports, 
business license applications, and public records requests. These are currently handled through phone calls, paper forms, emails to individual 
departments, and disconnected web forms, leading to lost requests, long processing times, inconsistent communications, and no transparency for 
citizens tracking their request status. Municipal staff in different departments use different systems, making inter-departmental coordination for 
complex requests difficult and time-consuming.
Objective:
Design a ServiceNow citizen services portal for local government that gives residents a single digital channel to submit all service requests, track 
real-time status, and receive proactive updates. Internally, the system should route requests intelligently to the appropriate departments, enforce 
SLAs, facilitate inter-departmental collaboration on complex requests, and provide city leadership with performance dashboards.
Elements to include in Design:
•Citizen Service Portal: Mobile-responsive self-service interface for submitting and tracking all service requests
•Service Catalog: Structured forms for permits, complaints, inspections, license applications, and public records requests
•Intelligent Routing: Business rules to automatically assign requests to the correct department and staff
•Inter-Departmental Collaboration: Shared case workspace for requests requiring multiple department coordination
•SLA Management: Response time commitments with automated escalation for overdue items
•Notifications: Proactive status updates to citizens at each processing milestone via email and SMS
•City Leadership Dashboard: Service volume by type, average processing times, SLA compliance, and department performance
•Generative AI Controller
•Virtual Agent Designer
